WHY THREE PIECES omarchy-shell is a single process hosting your bar, notifications, polkit and lock screen. A plugin is QML inside it — so a speech model, CUDA and a microphone reader cannot go there. Omavoi is therefore a daemon that does the work, plus a thin plugin that draws it.
