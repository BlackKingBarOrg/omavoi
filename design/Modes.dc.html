Adding an LLM step more than doubles the wait. That is why it is per-mode and not global — Terminal stays at 0.3s, Prose can afford 0.7s.
An LLM step can fail or stall. On timeout the chain falls through to the previous stage’s text and injects that — a slow model degrades your dictation, it never swallows it.
